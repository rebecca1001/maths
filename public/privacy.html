Privacy

Mission Multiply privacy policy · Last updated 1 October 2026

Students play under made-up space names. We store no student emails, photos, birthdays or addresses. All data is stored in Canada, there are no ads, and we never sell information.

Mission Multiply, 3868 12th Avenue, Smithers, BC V0J 2N0, Canada. Questions: footiejack2021@gmail.com.

Who uses it

Students in grades 3 to 7 practise times tables, and their teachers track progress. Schools and school districts decide to use Mission Multiply; we act on their behalf and follow their instructions about student information.

What we collect about students

A made-up space name the app generates, a 4-digit login code, the class they belong to, and how they play: which multiplication and division facts they answered, whether they were right and how many seconds they took, rounds played, stars, characters and badges.

If the teacher chooses, the student's real name, so teachers can tell who is who. Only teachers at the school can see it, and it is never shown in the game.

We do not collect students' email addresses, photos, birthdays, addresses, location or contacts.

What we collect about teachers

Name, school email address, password (stored scrambled by our database provider, never readable by us), and the school they belong to.

How we use it

Only to run the game (choosing questions, rewards, ranks) and to show teachers how their students are doing. We never sell information, show ads, build advertising profiles, or use student information for anything other than providing Mission Multiply to the school.

Who can see it

  • A student sees their own progress, and their classmates' space names, characters, and stars and minutes played this week (for the class Top 10).
  • Teachers see only students at their own school, including any real names they add.
  • Our small team can access data only to provide support or fix problems.

Where it is stored

In Canada, with our database provider Supabase, in its Canada (Central) region. Our website host serves the app's files and may keep standard web logs (such as IP addresses) for security for a short time. The app has no ads, analytics, trackers or third-party scripts.

How long we keep it

  • While a class is active.
  • When a teacher archives a class, students can no longer log in, and the class and its students' data are deleted 12 months later.
  • A teacher can remove a student at any time, which deletes their data straight away.
  • A teacher can delete their own account at any time from the Settings tab.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database rules stop teachers seeing other schools, and stop the public seeing any data. Repeated wrong login codes pause logins for that class.

Your choices

Parents and guardians can ask their child's teacher, or us at footiejack2021@gmail.com, to see or delete their child's data. Teachers can download their class's data at any time. Schools and districts can ask us to export or delete all of their data.

Changes

We will tell schools before we make any important change to this policy.
